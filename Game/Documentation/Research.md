# Research

Research upgrades currently defined in game data.

## Access Requirement

- Requires shop purchase: `Access to the Public Library`.

## Upgrades

| Upgrade | Credits Cost | Research Cost | Effect | Additional Unlock Requirements |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Pickaxe Training Program | 350 | 0 | Coal Mine output `+20%` | At least `1x Coal Mine` built |
| Improved Drill Heads | 700 | 0 | Coal Mine speed `+20%` | At least `2x Coal Mine` built, and `120 coal` gathered (lifetime) |
| Ore Silo Expansion | 1100 | 0 | Warehouse planning upgrade placeholder (future storage cap bonus) | At least `2x Warehouse` built, and `80 wood` gathered (lifetime) |
| Belt Conveyor Retrofit | 1700 | 0 | Sawmill speed `+20%` | At least `1x Sawmill` built, and `60 lumber` gathered (lifetime) |
| Smelter Insulation | 2400 | 0 | Power Plant output `+15%` | At least `1x Power Plant` built, and `300 energy` gathered (lifetime) |
| Automatic Shift Supervisor | 3200 | 30 | Automation unlock placeholder | At least `2x Coal Mine`, `2x Woodcutter`, `1x Sawmill` built, plus lifetime gathered: `coal 250`, `wood 200`, `lumber 120`, `energy 500` |

## Notes

- Some research effects are marked as placeholders and may be partially or not yet simulated.
- "Gathered" means lifetime produced (not current storage).
